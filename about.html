Meet Douglas Colvin

Douglas is a delivery-focused Programme and Portfolio Director with 25+ years leading high-impact, full-lifecycle programmes for blue-chip clients worldwide — spanning business change, systems development, data migration, and infrastructure capability, from AI supercomputing platforms to core banking transformation.

He has commanded programme and project budgets of up to $1B and matrix-managed teams of 100+ across multiple vendors, setting the governance, standards, and processes that lift delivery performance and communicating with confidence at Board and C-suite level.

An early adopter of agentic AI, Douglas designs and deploys autonomous AI agents using the full Claude suite of tools to automate delivery, reporting, and data analysis — the founding idea behind P3MAI.
