The Problem We Solve

Boards across the Gulf are approving AI and transformation investment faster than their delivery organizations can absorb it. The pattern is familiar: the business case is sound, the vendors are signed, and eighteen months later the program is amber-going-red, the reporting says otherwise, and nobody in the room can say precisely where the money went.

P3MAI exists for that gap. Douglas Colvin has spent 25 years closing it — across banking, aviation, telecoms, energy, shipping and retail, and most recently at the sharp end of sovereign AI: two TOP500-ranked supercomputers delivered inside US export-control constraints, and the Responsible AI PMO built from the ground up at Core42/G42. The method is not mysterious: governance that surfaces truth early, reporting a Board can act on, vendors held to account — now accelerated by agentic AI that automates the PMO machinery itself.

The upside is the one that matters at Board level: decisions made on facts, value delivered earlier, and a program brought to a controlled, value-driven close — with the capability left behind, not walked out the door.
