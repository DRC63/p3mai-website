Program Management

End-to-end ownership of the initiatives that cannot be allowed to fail — from Board mandate to controlled, value-driven close.

When You Call

The moments this service exists for.

  • The Board has approved a strategic investment — AI, infrastructure, transformation — and needs one accountable owner, not a committee.
  • A flagship program is amber-going-red, the reporting says otherwise, and nobody can say precisely where the money went.
  • Multiple vendors each own a piece; no one owns the outcome.
  • The initiative touches regulation, security, or sovereignty — and "we'll work it out as we go" is not an acceptable answer.

How It Works

One senior, accountable program leader takes ownership of the outcome — the same person the CV describes, not a rotating bench. The method is governance that surfaces truth early: a reporting line the Board can act on, vendors held to commercial account, risks worked before they become headlines, and a delivery cadence that trades drama for evidence. MSP-governed, Agile, Waterfall or hybrid — the framework serves the program, never the other way round.

The upside: capital deployed with control, value realized earlier and visibly, and an organization that finishes stronger than it started — because the governance, standards and reporting stay behind when the engagement ends.

Proven Where It's Hardest

Sovereign AI, delivered under sanction-grade scrutiny

The Problem

Build some of the world's fastest AI infrastructure — in the Gulf, on restricted US-origin technology, inside secure NOC and SCIF environments, with the TOP500 clock running.

The Approach

Full program ownership across vendors, facilities, compliance and cybersecurity — governance tight enough to satisfy export-control obligations, pragmatic enough to keep 1,272-GPU clusters on schedule.

The Upside

Two TOP500-ranked machines — #25 globally and fastest in EMEA (2024), then #20 globally (2025) — delivered secure, compliant and operational at Core42/G42. That is what "AI-enabled, sovereign and safe" means when it has to survive an audit.
