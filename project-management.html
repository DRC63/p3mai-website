Project Management

Hands-on delivery of the projects your business actually feels — delivered, recovered, or accelerated.

When You Call

The moments this service exists for.

  • A regulatory, security, or revenue-critical project has a date that will not move — and a plan that will not survive contact.
  • A project in flight has lost its way: scope creeping, vendors drifting, steering packs getting longer and saying less.
  • The delivery method itself is the problem — Waterfall grinding where the business needs value incrementally, or "agile" with no discipline underneath.
  • You need someone who can run onshore and offshore teams to a hard deadline without burning the relationship with the business.

How It Works

Full lifecycle ownership, hands on: plan, risks, vendors, testing, cutover. PRINCE2 discipline where control matters, Agile where the business needs value sooner, and the judgement — earned across nine industries — to know which is which. Risk and issue management is run as a working tool, not a compliance artifact; suppliers are coordinated to one plan; and the reporting tells the sponsor the truth while there is still time to act on it.

The upside: a project that lands — on the date, in control, with the business still on side — and a delivery team that finishes more capable than it started.

Proven Where It's Hardest

Real-time fraud defence, inside a live bank

The Problem

Deliver a real-time fraud management engine into Emirates NBD's live customer channels — where a bad release is front-page news and every integration touches regulated systems.

The Approach

Led delivery of the SAS fraud engine into the bank's backend and customer-facing channels — and shifted the delivery method itself from Waterfall to Agile, so protection reached customers incrementally instead of in one distant big bang. Alongside it: a new PEP-screening platform and a call-centre technical refresh across UAE and KSA with no interruption to service.

The Upside

Real-time fraud detection and prevention live across the bank's channels, benefits arriving sooner with every increment, and compliance capability strengthened — delivered inside one of the UAE's largest banks without disturbing the business it protects.
